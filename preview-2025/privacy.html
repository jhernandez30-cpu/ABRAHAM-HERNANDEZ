Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Josue Abraham Hernandez collects, uses, protects, retains, and deletes information related to requests and contracts for web development, software development, cybersecurity, digital automation, AI automation, and technology consulting services in Nicaragua.

This document is designed to follow good personal data protection practices applicable in Nicaragua, including Law No. 787, Personal Data Protection Law, when applicable. It is informational and does not replace specialized legal advice.

1. Service provider

The provider is Josue Abraham Hernandez, a technology service provider in Nicaragua. Requests related to this policy can be made through the contact channels available at contacto.html.

2. Data collected when requesting or hiring services

Depending on the request, the following information may be collected:

  • Identification and contact data, such as name, company, email, phone number, and preferred channel.
  • Commercial data, such as project scope, budget range, deadlines, requirements, quotes, and invoices.
  • Technical data, such as domain, hosting, repositories, APIs, system architecture, logs, diagnostics, and error reports.
  • Project information, such as content, designs, credentials provided by the client, documentation, databases, and internal processes.
  • Information submitted through contact forms, email, WhatsApp, calls, meetings, repositories, or third-party platforms.

3. Use of contact forms

Contact forms are used to receive inquiries, prepare quotes, answer requests, schedule meetings, provide support, and follow up on services. The client should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through public forms.

4. Sensitive client information and confidentiality

Some projects may require access to sensitive technical, commercial, or operational information. Such information is handled only for the agreed purpose and is treated as confidential. Project details, credentials, source code, databases, architecture, security findings, and internal business information are not disclosed unless the client authorizes it or a legal obligation requires it.

5. Purposes of processing

Information may be used to:

  • Prepare quotes, proposals, technical scopes, contracts, and implementation plans.
  • Provide web development, software, cybersecurity, automation, support, and consulting services.
  • Communicate progress, incidents, approvals, deliverables, changes, and support responses.
  • Manage billing, accounting records, payment follow-up, and administrative documentation.
  • Improve the quality, security, maintainability, and performance of the delivered solutions.
  • Comply with applicable obligations and respond to legitimate requests from the data subject.

6. Information security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are applied to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. These measures may include restricted access, secure storage, password management, backups, code review, encrypted connections when available, and separation of production and test environments when the project requires it.

7. Client responsibility when sharing access

The client is responsible for sharing only authorized access, credentials, systems, accounts, repositories, data, and internal information. The client must ensure that it has permission to provide such access and should revoke or rotate credentials when a service ends or when security risk requires it.

8. External tools and service providers

Projects may require external tools such as hosting providers, domain registrars, analytics tools, email services, repositories, APIs, cloud services, payment processors, security tools, AI platforms, or project management systems. These providers process information according to their own policies and terms. Only the information necessary for the service should be shared with them.

9. Data subject rights

The user or client may request access, correction, update, deletion, limitation, or review of their personal data when applicable. Requests will be evaluated according to the nature of the service, contractual obligations, legal retention requirements, and the rights recognized by applicable Nicaraguan data protection rules, including Law No. 787 when applicable.

10. Retention and deletion

Information is retained for the time necessary to provide the service, document the relationship, comply with billing or legal obligations, provide support, and protect legitimate interests. When information is no longer needed, it may be deleted, anonymized, archived, or returned to the client, depending on the project and the agreed conditions.

11. Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect operational, technical, legal, or service changes. The current version will be published on this page.

12. Privacy contact

To make privacy-related requests, use the contact channels published at contacto.html and include enough information to identify the request.
